# AGENTS.md

## Public documentation rules

- Keep every document product-facing, concise, and safe for public release.
- Describe the product goal, reader problem, product model, and public trust principles.
- Do not claim or imply a development stage, launch state, release date, or current availability.
- Do not publish source code, executable pseudocode, technology choices, system architecture, vendors, credentials, security controls, private research, internal URLs, operational records, or the private roadmap.
- Treat private repositories and branches as source material only. Rewrite allowed product intent for the public audience instead of copying internal records.
- Keep implementation-neutral language. Describe what vox is designed to do, not how a private system does it.
- Keep internal decision dates, review dates, ticket history, and delivery chronology out of public copy. Git history records publication chronology.
- Use Story as the primary unit of explanation. Keep evidence, perspectives, public reaction, and updates distinct.
- Never use em dash or en dash characters. Use commas, colons, or separate sentences.
- Apply the `unslop` skill to every public draft.
- Scan every Markdown file for forbidden dash characters and confidential implementation terms before commit.
