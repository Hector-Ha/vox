<h1>Product boundaries</h1>

<strong>Clear non-goals keep vox focused on reader understanding.</strong>

Clear non-goals make the vox idea easier to judge. They also protect the product from becoming a generic answer machine with a social-media skin.

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>What vox does</strong><br><br>Help a reader understand one public situation through a structured, source-backed Story.</td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>What vox refuses to become</strong><br><br>A verdict label, opinion poll, surveillance dashboard, moderation tool, reply network, or general chatbot.</td>
  </tr>
</table>

## vox is not a verdict label

Some Stories contain checkable claims. Others concern sequence, missing background, conflicting interpretations, or a public dispute whose facts are only part of the problem.

vox can explain why a claim is unsupported without reducing every Story to true or false.

## vox is not a public-opinion poll

Retrieved reactions are a bounded sample of visible material. They may reveal positions and reasons. They do not automatically represent a platform, region, or public.

vox names the boundary or avoids the number.

## vox is not a social-listening dashboard

The product is built for reader understanding, not brand surveillance, audience targeting, trend trading, or reputation scoring.

## vox is not a moderation system

vox can explain a disputed post and handle problems inside its own Stories. It does not decide which public posts a platform should allow, rank, or remove.

## vox is not another public comment network

Reader Contributions can inform later Story analysis, but vox does not publish them as a new reply feed. The point is to improve the explanation, not create another arena for the same argument.

## vox is not a general chatbot

vox answers through a structured Story tied to public material. It is not designed for arbitrary conversation, personal advice, or answers detached from inspectable sources.

## vox does not promise complete internet coverage

Private groups, closed accounts, deleted material, personalized feeds, and inaccessible platforms create real gaps. vox should narrow its claims when coverage is incomplete. It should never imply that unseen communities were measured.

## vox does not publish certainty on demand

A reader request creates a reason to investigate. It does not create evidence. When the material cannot support a useful Story, the responsible product outcome is no Story.
